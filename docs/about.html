About CreedaVA

Founded in 2023, CreedaVA is a Belize-based virtual assistant agency with a mission to bridge opportunity and talent. In a nation where education continues to rise but employment remains scarce, we serve as a digital bridge — connecting Belize’s highly skilled, bilingual professionals with global businesses.

Belize, the only English-speaking country in Central America, is uniquely positioned at the crossroads of cultures. Our team is fluent in English and Spanish, and rich in adaptability, communication, and professionalism.

Our Mission

To bridge opportunity and talent by connecting Belize’s highly skilled workforce with global businesses, creating sustainable remote career pathways while delivering exceptional virtual assistant services that empower companies to achieve more.

Our Vision

CreedaVA is more than a service — it’s a movement to empower Belize’s workforce, foster remote career growth, and create sustainable pathways for global collaboration. By hiring through CreedaVA, companies invest in a vision of progress, purpose, and partnership.

Our Approach

We take a partnership approach with every client. From our initial discovery call through ongoing support, we invest time in understanding your business, your challenges, and your goals. We’re not just completing tasks — we’re helping you build systems that scale.
