Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working with CreedaVA. Can’t find the answer? Contact us.

What is a virtual assistant?

A virtual assistant (VA) is a skilled remote professional who provides administrative, technical, or creative support to businesses. Our VAs work from Belize and are available during your business hours, providing the same level of support as an in-office employee.

How do I get started?

Simply schedule a free consultation through our contact page. We’ll discuss your needs, match you with the right VA, and get you started within 48 hours. No long-term contracts required.

What tasks can a VA handle?

Our VAs can handle a wide range of tasks including email management, calendar scheduling, data entry, customer support, social media management, bookkeeping, research, and more. Check our Services page for a full list.

Are your VAs bilingual?

Yes! Belize is the only English-speaking country in Central America, and our team is fluent in both English and Spanish. This makes us ideally suited for businesses serving diverse markets.

How do you ensure data security?

We take data security seriously. All VAs sign strict NDAs, we use encrypted communication channels, and we follow enterprise-grade security protocols. Your business information is always protected.

What are your business hours?

Our standard hours align with US business hours (EST/CST), but we offer flexible scheduling including 24/7 availability for Premium and Enterprise plans. We’ll work with you to find the best schedule.

Can I change my plan?

Absolutely. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time with 30 days notice. We understand that business needs change and we’re flexible.

Is there a setup fee?

No. There are no setup fees, onboarding charges, or hidden costs. You pay the monthly rate and that’s it. We believe in transparent pricing.

What if I’m not satisfied?

Your satisfaction is our priority. If you’re not happy with your VA, we’ll rematch you with a new one at no extra cost. We also offer a satisfaction guarantee for the first 30 days.

Do you offer specialized VAs?

Yes! We have VAs with expertise in real estate, insurance processing, bookkeeping, marketing, and more. During your consultation, we’ll identify the right specialist for your industry.

Still Have Questions?

Our team is happy to help. Reach out and we’ll get back to you within 24 hours.
