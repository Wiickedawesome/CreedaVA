Bookings: Fueling Your Growth Pipeline

Understanding your booking funnel is crucial for scaling. Real-time data helps you identify bottlenecks and optimize conversion paths.

Key Booking Metrics:

Booking Conversion Rate

Percentage of leads that successfully convert into a booked consultation or service.

Average Booking Value (ABV)

Average monetary value of each new booking, indicating plan popularity.

Booking Velocity

Time taken from initial lead contact to a confirmed booking, highlighting sales efficiency.

Lead Source Performance

Which channels are driving the most, and highest quality, bookings.

Expected Impact:

  • Optimize sales funnel to increase booking conversion by ~15%.
  • Improve resource allocation by identifying high-performing lead sources.
  • Reduce booking cancellation by ~10% through optimized follow-ups.

Marketing Campaigns: Optimizing Every Dollar

Are your marketing efforts truly paying off? Data-driven campaign optimization ensures every dollar spent translates into measurable results.

Essential Campaign Metrics:

Return on Ad Spend (ROAS)

Direct revenue generated for every dollar spent on a specific campaign.

Cost Per Lead (CPL)

The cost incurred to acquire one new lead, indicating campaign efficiency.

Campaign Conversion Rate

Percentage of clicks or impressions that result in a desired action (e.g., form submission).

Customer Lifetime Value (LTV) by Source

Understanding which campaigns attract the most valuable, long-term customers.

Expected Impact:

  • Increase ROAS by ~20% through real-time budget reallocation.
  • Reduce CPL by ~15% by pausing underperforming ads.
  • Improve overall campaign effectiveness by ~25% with data-driven audience targeting.

Customer Health: Proactive Retention & Expansion

Retaining and growing existing clients is more cost-effective than acquiring new ones. Visibility into customer health allows for proactive support and strategic upsells.

Critical Customer Metrics:

Churn Risk Score

A predictive score based on engagement, task completion rates, and feedback to identify at-risk clients.

Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)

Total revenue expected from a customer throughout their relationship with CreedaVA.

Customer Satisfaction Score (CSAT/NPS)

Direct feedback from clients on their happiness and willingness to recommend.

Upsell/Cross-sell Rate

Percentage of existing clients who upgrade their service or purchase additional services.

Expected Impact:

  • Proactively reduce churn by ~10-15% through early intervention.
  • Identify upsell opportunities to increase ARPA by ~8%.
  • Boost customer satisfaction, leading to more referrals and stronger brand loyalty.
